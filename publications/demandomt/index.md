## Overview

**DemandOMT** studies a simple question: can a robot navigate from what a person *needs*, rather than from an explicitly named object?

The framework uses a vision-language model to infer an object goal from an implicit human demand and visual observations, then passes the predicted goal to a learned object-navigation policy. The work also examines grounding errors, candidate constraints, and termination behaviour in the end-to-end pipeline.
