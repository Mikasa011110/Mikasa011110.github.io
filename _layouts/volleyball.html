Beyond research · Volleyball

Volleyball has been part of my life for a decade.

I started playing at school in Nanjing and kept carrying the sport with me through university in Suzhou and Liverpool. The teams changed, but what drew me back to the court stayed the same: timing, trust, communication, and the feeling of six people learning to move as one.

Playing since 2015
Positions Outside Hitter · Libero
Places Nanjing · Suzhou · Liverpool

Why I play

A game of reading, reacting, and trusting.

Volleyball is fast, collective, and unforgiving of hesitation. A point can change in a fraction of a second, and no player can control the rally alone. I enjoy that dependence on awareness and teamwork: reading the court, making a decision, and trusting the next touch.

Over the years, the sport also became a way to meet people and stay grounded outside study and research. Some of my strongest memories are not individual points, but the routines around them — training together, competing together, and learning how different teammates communicate under pressure.

My journey

Four stages, one sport.

A short timeline of the teams and places that shaped my experience on court.

01

2015–2016

Nanjing Foreign Language School

I first started playing volleyball in 2015. The following year, I became captain of the school men’s team. It was my first experience of discovering how much a team depends on communication as much as individual technique.

02

2017–2021

NFLS Xianlin Campus

I continued playing throughout my secondary-school years and served as team captain. During this period, I regularly competed in the Nanjing Sunshine Sports Volleyball Competition and built the habits of training, preparation, and team responsibility that stayed with me later.

03

2021–2023

Xi’an Jiaotong–Liverpool University

At XJTLU, I played for the men’s volleyball team and served as captain. I also helped organise training and instruction within the club, which gave me a different perspective on how a team grows beyond match day.

04

2023–2025

University of Liverpool

Moving to the UK meant joining a new team and adapting to a different playing environment. I played for the University of Liverpool men’s first team for two seasons, which became one of the most memorable parts of my undergraduate years.

On the court

Two positions, two ways of seeing the rally.

I have spent most of my time as an outside hitter, while also playing libero. I enjoy the contrast: one role asks me to contribute across serve receive, transition, and attack; the other places even more emphasis on reading the game and keeping the next touch possible.

01

Outside Hitter

Serve receive · transition · left-side attack · coverage

02

Libero

Reading attacks · back-court organisation · first-contact quality

What stays with me

The best rallies are rarely the work of one person. They are built one touch at a time, with each player making the next action possible.

That is probably what I value most about volleyball: it rewards awareness, adaptability, and trust — qualities that remain useful far beyond the court.
